I enjoy making technology feel clear and useful — from the first wireframe to the project documentation that helps bring it to life.

My approach

As a fourth-year Computer Engineering student based in Rizal, Philippines, I’m especially drawn to UI/UX design. I like learning what people need, organizing information into clear user journeys, and shaping those ideas into thoughtful wireframes and prototypes.

I also value the work behind a polished experience: documenting decisions clearly, sharing ideas with teammates, and refining details through feedback. My goal is to make digital products feel intuitive, inclusive, and easy to use.
